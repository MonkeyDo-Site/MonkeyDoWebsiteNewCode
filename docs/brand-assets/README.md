# MonkeyDo original brand assets

The owner identified **Final MonkeyDo** as the authoritative logo and **MonkeyDo Ad** as advertisement/reference material, and reattached a standalone logo, two advertisements and facility photographs during reconstruction. Photos show climbing/slide/ball and foam pits, toddler/swing area, imaginative camp/fishing/café, game room and entrance.

## Preservation and intended use

- Preserve the logo exactly. Do not redraw, regenerate, recreate or alter it.
- Use original ads for visual direction: sage/coral/taupe/cream, organic thick-bordered frames, playful rounded typography, restrained jungle character. The owner approved ad photograph use and supplied facility photos for the website.
- Older ad wording, summer/after-Sukkos statements, therapists claim or incomplete package descriptions do not supersede later written requirements.
- The approved About areas remain **Imaginative Play, Game Room, Climbing Area, Toddler Zone**. Do not replace this inventory with different assistant photo categories. Additional program-specific imagery is not required.
- Final crops/layout and short accessible image descriptions are staging choices; alt text is not replacement marketing copy. Avoid hiding the relevant play feature in mobile crops.
